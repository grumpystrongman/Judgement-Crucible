# Pilot and Go-to-Market Readiness

## Positioning

**Category:** facilitated executive cyber judgment simulation.

**One-line promise:** Put cyber leaders under realistic decision pressure before the real incident does.

**Differentiator:** The product captures a defensible executive commitment — action, protected priority, accepted tradeoff, trigger, and owner — after exposing disagreement but before consensus can erase it.

## Ideal first buyers

- CISO leadership-development programs
- cyber executive education providers
- security consulting firms
- enterprise security leadership off-sites
- first-100-days / aspiring-CISO programs
- cyber-risk executive workshops

The first sales motion should be a facilitated experience, not a self-serve software subscription. Facilitation is part of what makes the product valuable while the scenario library is small.

## Pilot offer

A commercially credible first offer can be:

**Judgment Crucible: The Trusted Path**

- 45-minute live session
- 6–12 leaders
- facilitator-led
- one shared display + participant phones
- four escalating calls
- Monday-after reveal
- 15-minute optional debrief extension
- team pattern summary, not individual grading

## Proof to capture during pilots

Capture evidence that supports buying decisions without violating participant privacy:

- completion rate,
- average room size,
- calls where initial judgment was most divided,
- percentage of Chairs who changed from their private initial choice,
- facilitator-reported discussion quality,
- participant-reported relevance to real executive work,
- buyer willingness to run another scenario/cohort.

Avoid publishing individual vote histories or turning pilots into covert performance assessments.

## Launch assets already represented in product

- commercial landing page,
- buyer-oriented explanation of the mechanic,
- privacy page,
- real QR join flow,
- facilitator console,
- room-scale cinematic presentation,
- responsive participant controller,
- deterministic simulator,
- automated product screenshots,
- security model and deployment documentation,
- production CI gate.

## Before broad launch

- [ ] Complete 3–5 facilitated pilots with different executive groups.
- [ ] Tighten any copy that repeatedly requires facilitator explanation.
- [ ] Confirm legal ownership/licensing of all scenario and brand assets.
- [ ] Add a production support/security contact.
- [ ] Add privacy policy/company identity appropriate to the selling entity.
- [ ] Add product analytics only after deciding exactly what may be collected.
- [ ] Establish retention/deletion policy for completed rooms.
- [ ] Configure custom domain, App Check, alerting, and Firebase budget controls.
- [ ] Decide whether buyers receive a post-session PDF/report and what it may contain.
- [ ] Price the facilitated experience before optimizing for self-serve onboarding.

## Product roadmap after launch evidence

The most valuable expansion is likely **scenario breadth**, not feature breadth. Add scenarios that create different executive tensions — third-party compromise, identity/control-plane breach, destructive attack, data exfiltration, insider ambiguity, AI/vendor exposure — while preserving the same familiar multiplayer shell.

Only after repeated buyers ask for it should the product expand into facilitator accounts, scenario libraries, cohort reporting, licensing controls, or AI-assisted debriefing.
